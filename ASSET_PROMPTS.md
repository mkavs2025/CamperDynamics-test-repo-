# V4 visual assets

Generated using the built-in image generator, with the supplied reference mockup as the composition/style reference. Final assets are copied into this repository and included in the downloadable source archive.

## Scenic exterior — dist/assets/hilux-lakeside.png

A realistic silver/white 2022-style Hilux double cab with a sand-beige raised flatbed pop-up camper, solar and deployed tan side awning. Front-left three-quarter view, parked on rocky grass beside a turquoise alpine lake, forested hills, mountains and cloudy blue sky. Natural subdued afternoon light, realistic automotive photography. Vehicle in the lower portion with spacious sky for text. No UI, labels, people or watermark. This is an illustrative lifestyle scene rather than the live selected configuration.

## Product atlas — dist/assets/accessories-atlas.png

Precisely regular four-column, three-row product-photo atlas with no text or borders, equally sized cells on near-black charcoal backgrounds. Row 1: tan awning, shower tent, storage organiser, solar panel. Row 2: warm-white LED light bar, black MOLLE panel, lockable storage hatch, indoor shower tray/curtain. Row 3: mosquito screen, modular kitchen/storage cabinet, annex room, portable ladder. Realistic isolated products, consistent three-quarter studio lighting, no logos.

## Scene thumbnails

The JPG interior, rear and cutaway assets are browser captures of the application's original 3D geometry. They are labelled as 3D views in the interface and switch to their corresponding real camera presets.
